# Planetia — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 1, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use **Planetia**, the Android game identified by the package name `com.hollowmindedmakers.planetia` (the **Game**).

Planetia is developed and published by **Grzegorz Swół**, using the developer name **Hollow Minded Makers** (referred to below as **we**, **us**, or **our**).

**Privacy contact:** [hollowmindedmakers@gmail.com](mailto:hollowmindedmakers@gmail.com)

This policy applies to Planetia. Other games published under the Hollow Minded Makers name may have their own privacy notices.

## 1. Information handled by Planetia

Planetia can be played using locally stored game data. Some optional or platform-dependent features involve Google services, as described below.

### Game progress and creations

The Game stores information such as settings, preferences, progress, saved planets, presets, and user-created content on your device. Photos, videos, and other files you choose to export are saved to the location supported or selected by your device. These files are not automatically sent to us merely because you create or export them.

If you use Planetia's **Google Play Games cloud-save features**, eligible saved-game data and related metadata may be transmitted to, stored by, and retrieved from Google's services so that you can synchronize or recover your progress. Cloud-save data may contain names or other content you choose to include in your creations.

### Google Play Games Services

When you use Google Play Games features, Google may process information associated with your Play Games profile, such as a player identifier and display name, together with achievements, gameplay statistics, and cloud-save information. Planetia uses the information made available by Google Play Games to provide the relevant features. Your broader Google account information is managed by Google under its own terms and privacy policy.

### Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed through **Google Play Billing**. To deliver and restore purchased content, the Game may handle information such as purchased product identifiers, purchase status, purchase tokens, and associated entitlements. Payment-card and other payment-method details are handled by Google; Planetia does not ask you to enter them into the Game.

### Optional usage analytics

Planetia includes **Google Analytics for Firebase** to help us understand how the Game is used and improve its reliability and design. Analytics collection is intended to operate subject to your choice in the Game's privacy or analytics settings.

When enabled, analytics may include gameplay events, interactions, session and engagement information, app version, device or operating-system characteristics, and identifiers used by Firebase or Google Analytics to associate events with an app installation. The exact identifiers and device information depend on the SDK configuration and your device or platform settings.

You can change your analytics preference in the Game's settings. Disabling analytics stops future optional analytics collection as implemented by the Game, but does not necessarily remove information already processed by Google. To ask about deletion of information associated with your use of Planetia, contact us using the address above.

### Platform diagnostics

Google Play and Android may provide developers with crash, performance, compatibility, and similar diagnostic information through their platform tools. Such information is handled under the relevant Google services' policies and may be presented to us in an aggregated or limited form.

## 2. Why information is used

We use or arrange processing of information only as relevant to the following purposes:

- **Providing the Game:** saving settings and progress, creating and exporting content, and delivering features you request.
- **Google Play Games features:** sign-in-related game functionality, achievements, statistics, and synchronization of eligible saves.
- **Purchases:** delivering purchases, restoring entitlements, and handling billing-related problems.
- **Optional analytics:** understanding feature usage and improving the Game when analytics collection is enabled.
- **Security and compliance:** protecting game services, investigating technical problems, responding to valid requests, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, the legal basis depends on the processing: providing requested game or purchase features may be necessary to perform a contract; optional analytics relies on consent where required; certain records may be necessary for legal compliance; and limited security or troubleshooting processing may be based on legitimate interests where permitted. You may withdraw consent for optional analytics without losing access to the core Game.

## 3. Service providers and sharing

Planetia uses the following Google services, depending on which features you enable or use:

| Service | Relevant purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Google Play Games Services | Player-related game features, achievements, statistics, and cloud saves |
| Google Play Billing | Processing and verifying in-app purchases |
| Google Analytics for Firebase | Optional usage and engagement analytics |
| Google Play / Android platform services | App distribution, updates, and platform diagnostics |

These services may process information on Google's infrastructure and under applicable Google terms. We do not sell personal information, and **Planetia does not display advertisements**.

You can learn about Google's handling of data at [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 4. Data storage and retention

- **Local data** stays on your device until you delete it, reset relevant settings, or remove the Game, subject to any device-level backups you have enabled.
- **Cloud saves** are kept through Google Play Games according to the cloud-save feature and Google's applicable policies until you delete them or otherwise arrange for their removal.
- **Purchases** and related transaction records may be retained by Google or us where necessary for purchase restoration, dispute handling, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.
- **Analytics information**, where collected, is retained in accordance with the retention configuration of the associated Google Analytics property and relevant Google service policies.

Information may be retained for longer where required by applicable law or to resolve a legitimate dispute, and is otherwise deleted or de-identified when no longer needed for its stated purpose.

## 5. Your choices and deletion requests

Depending on your device and available Game features, you can:

- Manage or remove local saved data within Planetia.
- Use the Game's cloud-save management controls to manage eligible Google Play Games saves.
- Change the optional analytics preference in Planetia's settings.
- Manage your Google Play Games profile and related account settings through Google.
- Contact us at [hollowmindedmakers@gmail.com](mailto:hollowmindedmakers@gmail.com) to ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of information related to Planetia.

We will review privacy requests in accordance with applicable law. Some data, such as Google account or payment information, is controlled directly through Google's services and may also need to be managed using your Google account settings.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with applicable privacy rights, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, to data portability where applicable, and to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to complain to a competent data-protection authority.

## 6. International processing and security

Google services may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Applicable legal protections and transfer mechanisms depend on the service and circumstances; further information is available in Google's privacy and data-protection documentation.

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the nature of the Game and rely on established platform services for the features described above. Nevertheless, no method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Children and families

Planetia is a creative game. The availability of particular features, including Google account features and purchases, may depend on age, parental controls, and the rules of the relevant platform. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that requires attention, please contact us.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Planetia's features or data practices change, or when legal requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel where required.
